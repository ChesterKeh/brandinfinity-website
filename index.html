      Services

      From first idea to launch day

      1. We connect you to key stakeholders in global entertainment IPs across film, gaming and streaming, with direct access through trusted relationships and regional expertise. We are your gateway agency at a local, regional and global level: the first step in your IP partnership journey.

        • Alliances with IP rights holders
        • Premiere sponsorship & events
        • Collaboration & partnership planning

      2. We evaluate your brand and objectives to shape the right entertainment strategy and advise on the best genre fits. We map partnership models and deal terms to the timings that fit your calendar goals and budget.

        • Strategic planning in entertainment
        • Genre & IP fit
        • Calendar & budget planning

      3. We negotiate across all product categories, so the deal structure fits your business and marketing needs.

        Licensed
        Fee-based, no media support.
        License-free
        Media and marketing support, no fees.
        Hybrid
        A mix of the two, built around your goals.
        • Licensing IP
        • Character licensing & product creation
        • Contract negotiation with rights holders

      4. We develop a framework across every brand and consumer touchpoint and support end-to-end campaign delivery.

        • Promotional programs
        • Sales mechanic activations
        • Social & digital
        • PR & launch events
        • E-commerce & in-trade
        • Product integration
        • Premiums & merchandise
        • In-film & in-game integration
